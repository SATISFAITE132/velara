# Velara — Brand Guidelines

## Positioning
Velara is a small-batch luxury hair oil brand built around one idea: hair care as a
daily ritual, not a chore. The line centres on 24-karat gold-flake elixirs and
plant-oil repair treatments, priced and presented like fine skincare rather than
mass-market hair product.

**Target customer:** 25–45, skincare-literate, already spends on "prestige" beauty,
shops Sephora/Cult Beauty/Net-a-Porter Beauty, follows hair stylists on Instagram.

**Tagline:** "Liquid Gold for Hair."

**Typography**
- Display: *Fraunces* (serif, optical sizing, used italic for hero moments) — carries
  the editorial, old-money warmth of the brand.
- Body/UI: *Inter* — a quiet, legible workhorse that never competes with Fraunces.

**Signature element — "The Pour":** a ribbon of gold that pours down and pools into a
droplet, used in the hero animation, as a section divider, and echoed in the hover
"oil sheen" sweep on product cards. This is the one motif the brand should always be
recognisable by, used sparingly.

**Logo concept:** Wordmark-only, set in Fraunces, wide letter-spacing (`VELARA`), no
icon lockup required at launch — the typography *is* the mark. If an icon is ever
introduced, it should be an abstracted droplet, not a bottle silhouette (too literal).

## Packaging
- **Primary bottle:** frosted amber glass, 50ml, brushed-gold dropper cap engraved
  with "V". Amber glass is functional (UV-protects the oils), not just aesthetic.
- **Secondary carton:** matte obsidian box, gold foil wordmark, cream interior tray.
- **Unboxing:** cotton-paper tissue in blush, a wax-sealed thank-you card, and a
  QR code linking to `/track-order` printed on the seal.
- **Sustainability note:** glass + FSC-certified card, refill pouches planned for the
  Gold Elixir bestseller as a phase-two SKU.
