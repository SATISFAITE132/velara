# Velara — Launch Marketing Strategy

## Phase 1 — Pre-launch (Weeks 1–4)
- Seed 40–60 mid-tier hairstylist/salon-owner accounts with the Discovery Trio in
  exchange for honest content — stylists are the brand's most credible validators.
- Build a waitlist landing page (reuse the Home hero) with a 15% "Circle" incentive
  (`WELCOME15` discount code already wired into the schema).
- Teaser content on Instagram/TikTok centred on "The Pour" motif — macro shots of
  the gold-flake oil pouring, matching the site's signature animation.

## Phase 2 — Launch (Weeks 5–8)
- Hero SKU push: Gold Elixir positioned as the "one bottle" hook, priced as a
  considered purchase ($68) rather than an impulse buy.
- Email flow: Welcome (15% off) → Ritual education (3-step routine from the Home
  page) → Bestseller social proof → Replenishment reminder at day 45.
- Paid: Meta/TikTok retargeting on video-view audiences from teaser content;
  influencer gifting tier at 10k–150k followers in the hair/skincare niche.
- Bundle economics: Discovery Trio priced to convert new customers at a lower
  barrier ($89 vs. $122 if bought separately) while lifting AOV once they return
  for full-size bottles.

## Phase 3 — Retention (Ongoing)
- Loyalty: points-per-dollar redeemable for the Amber Shine Drops (low COGS, high
  perceived value — good reward-tier economics).
- UGC: incentivize before/after content with a branded hashtag; feature top posts
  on the homepage testimonial marquee.
- Subscription: "Replenish & Save" — 10% recurring discount on the 45–60 day cycle
  matching typical usage of a 50ml bottle.

## Channel Mix & Success Metrics
| Channel | Role | KPI |
|---|---|---|
| Organic social (IG/TikTok) | Brand world, "The Pour" motif | Reach, saves |
| Stylist seeding | Credibility | UGC volume, referral codes redeemed |
| Email/SMS | Retention, AOV | Repeat purchase rate, flow revenue % |
| Paid social | Acquisition | CAC vs. $58 target AOV |
| Site (this build) | Conversion | Checkout completion rate, cart abandonment |

## Admin dashboard tie-in
The Analytics tab (`/admin`) tracks the exact KPIs this strategy depends on —
revenue trend, category mix, and order volume — so performance against the plan
above is visible from day one rather than bolted on later.
